## About

12 years in technology, in three acts:

- **Developer** — started at T-Systems writing C#, PHP and SQL; one C# automation turned a ~5-day SAP-to-PowerPoint reporting process into 10 minutes.
- **Data & BI** — years of enterprise data work at Itaú, Porto Seguro, Dell and Bosch: ETL pipelines, executive dashboards, and running Bosch's Tableau platform.
- **Product** — since 2022, building products. Today I work as an independent full stack engineer with TypeScript, React, Node.js, Python and Supabase, using AI-assisted development every day.

Before going independent, I was one of two developers who built a personal finance platform for a US client at LowCode Agency — I owned the PostgreSQL data model, and we shipped in about 6 months against a 12-month estimate.

B2C vehicle inspection report platform, built single-handedly for a client — **63,000+ reports sold**. Version 1 runs on Bubble with e-invoicing (Focus NFe) and payment (Efí) integrations. I'm now rebuilding it in code (v2) with admin, customer and affiliate portals.

### Lead Engine · in production (internal tool)

Internal admin tool at OAUTOVERSO, my own automotive platform: a Python pipeline that finds automotive businesses in Brazil's Federal Revenue open data and classifies them in three tiers: CNAE codes, then rules, then AI for the ambiguous cases — with autonomous decisions made by [Jev](https://typesafe.ai/blog/introducing-system-one-models-and-jev), TypeSafe AI's structured-decision model. Idempotent upserts by CNPJ, LGPD-driven data minimization, and a React/Node app on a VPS with 2FA. Internal by design, so there is no public link.

Next.js · TypeScript · Supabase · PostgreSQL · Zod — [case study →](https://silviomonteiro.dev/en/projects/vamo-ensaiar)

## How I work

- **Data first.** The data model is the first deliverable — everything else sits on top of it.
- **Secure by default.** Row-Level Security, JWT auth with MFA, and LGPD-compliant data handling from day one, not as an afterthought.
- **AI-assisted, human-owned.** I use Claude Code, Cursor and Codex daily to move faster, and LLMs in production where they earn their place — with evaluation, not vibes.
- **Close to the business.** I work directly with clients, from requirements gathering to production maintenance.
